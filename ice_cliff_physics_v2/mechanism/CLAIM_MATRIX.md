## Comparators needed for a professional-model claim

1. **Original coupled model and targeted ablations:** same forcing, geometry, footprints and calibration budget. Separate the effect of resolved normal conduction, wet resistance, wet/dry partition, local transfer law and height-dependent geometry. These comparisons establish which implementation feature matters; they are not independent environmental truth.
2. **Independent normal thermal reference:** compare against analytic solutions and a separately discretized slab while retaining the same water exchange. This verifies thermal numerics, not the common exchange closure.
3. **Independent hydraulics/phase-change reference:** the relevant two-/three-dimensional flow, wetting and melt calculation must itself pass its scope-specific verification and refinement gates. Reusing the same effective hydraulic rating cannot independently validate that rating.
4. **Established heat-transfer laws:** standard Dittus–Boelter and Gnielinski with declared applicability, plus equally trained constant-transfer and simple statistical baselines. These are component comparators; they are not complete glacier forecast models.
5. **Published cliff energy/evolution models:** evaluate the actual matching published formulation. Pinned public CliffEBM.R is a gridded atmospheric example, not a demonstrated reproduction of the entire Buri2016 dynamic pond/reburial model. A wet-bare proxy and an intentionally misapplied dry-only port must not be labelled all professional models.
6. **Independent field observations:** measured retreat/melt and compatible water/air/terrain/debris inputs, matched frame, interval and physical footprint. Sensor pressure is full flow depth only if clearance is known; sensor temperature at the bed is not automatically bulk-water temperature. Graphical error ranges, engineering scenarios and statistical confidence intervals remain separate.

## Paper decision rule

The strongest present defensible narrative is **a verified computational framework that distinguishes hydraulic access, thermal access and nonuniform ice retreat**, with a bounded application hypothesis about when one is an unreliable proxy for the other. It becomes a consequential environmental result only after its magnitude, prevalence and independence from assumptions are demonstrated.

Report the frozen campaign whether it passes or fails. Do not compensate for weak results by describing a constitutive identity as a newly discovered law, changing the target, suppressing a strong baseline, or interpreting supported components as a fully validated glacier model.
