Metadata for point mass balance derived from ablation stake sites on Kennicott Glacier, AlaskaCombination of data collected by:    University of Alaska Fairbanks (UAF)    Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS)    National Park Service (NPS)Data for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.Data fields include the mass change derived over the period between date_start and date_end, as well as measured positioning and snow cover variables for those start and end dates. Each row includes the mass change between two consecutive field measurements which may be between 1 to 12 months apart. Measurements were typically taken twice a year (spring/early summer and fall) with occasional additional measurements at some locations in mid summer. Thus, some data are duplicated since the end date of a reported mass change may be the start date for the next period.Mass changes are in all cases based strictly on stake-verified changes measured at a site between the indicated dates, and have not been adjusted or corrected to inferred seasonal mass maxima or minima. This data table also includes measurements of snow depth in spring/early summer at times/sites when no prior stake measurement close to the end of the previous mass-balance year is available. At these snow depth-only sites, the "date_start" is recorded as "unknown" and "date_end" is the date of snow depth measurement. In these cases the snow depth is not converted into a mass change since the exact period of mass change is unknown.DATA FIELDS:site: name of ablation stake site where point mass balance was calculateddate_start: start date of mass balance period, in YYYY-mm-dd format. If "unknown," this indicates a snow depth-only measurement taken on "date_end."date_end: end date of mass balance period, in YYYY-mm-dd formatperiod_length: length of mass balance period, in number of daysmass_change_kg_m2: mass balance over the period of measurement, reported in kg/m2 (equivalent to mm water equivalent)debris_thick_mm: surface debris thickness reported in mmlat_start: latitude of site in degrees N measured on date_startlon_start: longitude of site in degrees W measured on date_startelevation_start: elevation in meters above ellipsoid (WGS84) measured on date_startsurface_type_start: surface type of the site on date_start (ice, debris, or firn). If seasonal snow (i.e. snow on top of the previous summer surface) is present, this is the surface underneath the seasonal snow. seasonal_snow_depth_start_mm: observed depth of seasonal snow cover (i.e. snow on top of previous the summer surface), measured in mm. Measured on date_start.seasonal_snow_density_start_kg_m3: mean density of seasonal snow cover in kg/m3 measured on date_startlat_end: latitude of site in degrees N measured on date_endlon_end: longitude of site in degrees W measured on date_endelevation_end: elevation in meters above ellipsoid (WGS84) measured on date_endsurface_type_end: surface type of the site on date_end (ice, debris, or firn). If seasonal snow (i.e. snow on top of the previous summer surface) is present, this is the surface underneath the seasonal snow.seasonal_snow_depth_end_mm: observed depth of seasonal snow cover (i.e. snow on top of previous the summer surface), measured in mm. Measured on date_end. This measurement includes any late summer snow accumulation (recorded separately in "summer_snow" fields).seasonal_snow_density_end_kg_m3: observed mean density of seasonal snow cover (column seasonal_snow_depth_end_mm) in kg/m3 measured on date_end. summer_snow_depth_end_mm: observed depth of late summer snow accumulation (new snow observed at the surface during late melt season measurements) in mm measured on date_end; only reported if date_end is in late summer.summer_snow_density_end_kg_m3: density of late summer snow accumulation (new snow observed at the surface during late melt season measurements) in kg/m3 measured on date_end; only reported if date_end is in late summer.institution: reporting institution responsible for the site (UAF, CAS, or NPS)
