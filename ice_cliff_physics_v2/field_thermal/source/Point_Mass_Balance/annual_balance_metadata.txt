Metadata for annual point balances measured at stake sites on Kennicott Glacier, AlaskaCombination of data collected by:    University of Alaska Fairbanks (UAF)    Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS)    National Park Service (NPS)Data for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.This file includes point balances derived from the same data as presented in the file “point_mass_balance.csv,” but restricted to periods that roughly span one mass-balance year. Additional variables measured at the start and end date of the balance are found in “point_mass_balance.csv”.DATA FIELDS:site: name of ablation stake site where point mass balance was calculateddate_start: start date of mass balance period, in YYYY-mm-dd format. date_end: end date of mass balance period, in YYYY-mm-dd formatperiod_length: length of mass balance period, in number of daysmass_change_kg_m2: mass balance over the period of measurement, reported in kg/m2 (equivalent to mm water equivalent)debris_thick_mm: surface debris thickness reported in mm (independent of any seasonal snow)lat: approximate latitude of site in degrees N (typically the spatial average of the start and end date positions, or most recent data) lon: approximate longitude of site in degrees W (typically the spatial average of the start and end date positions, or most recent data)elevation: approximate elevation in meters above ellipsoid (WGS84) (typically the spatial average of the start and end date positions, or most recent data)institution: reporting institution responsible for the site (UAF, CAS, or NPS)
