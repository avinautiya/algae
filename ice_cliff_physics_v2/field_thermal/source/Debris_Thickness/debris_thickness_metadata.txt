Debris Thickness Measurements on Kennicott Glacier, AlaskaData for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.

Data is available as a comma-separated values (csv) file as well as a Geopackage file.Data collected byEric Petersen, University of Alaska Fairbanks (UAF)

Summary of Data Fields:lat: approximate latitude of site in degrees Nlon: approximate longitude of site in degrees Welevation: approximate elevation in meters above ellipsoid (WGS84) (typically the spatial average of the start and end date positions, or most recent data)date: date of measurement, in YYYY-mm-dd format. debris_thickness_cm: measured debris thickness, in cm. Estimated uncertainty in measurement
of ±2 cm.clast_description: qualitative description of the size of clasts making up the debris layer.context: description of local topographic/geomorphic context where the debris thickness measurement was made.
