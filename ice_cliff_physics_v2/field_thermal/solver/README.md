# Boundary-forced conduction diagnostic

This component solves T_t=alpha T_zz for constant effective diffusivity. The primary temperature experiment uses the shallowest/deepest *real* probes as Dirichlet endpoints and scores strictly interior probes. All first-row probes initialize a piecewise-linear profile once. Subsequent interior observations never enter the solver; there is no test-boundary reset. Endpoints are linearly interpolated over supported intervals only. Missing/nonfinite values, gaps and extrapolation are refused. Negative rock temperatures are retained; no phase-change or latent heat is represented.

Write xi=(z-z0)/L, T=(1-xi)Ttop+xi Tbottom+sum(v_n sin(n*pi*xi)). Each mode obeys v'_n=-alpha(n*pi/L)^2 v_n-ell'_n, with ell_n=2[Ttop+(-1)^(n+1)Tbottom]/(n*pi). A linear boundary interval therefore has the exact recurrence v_next=exp(-a)v-[(1-exp(-a))/a] Delta ell. Uniform intervals use the identical recurrence through scipy.signal.lfilter. Initial modal coefficients integrate the piecewise-linear initial profile analytically. First-row displayed temperatures equal the supplied initial interpolation; modal reconstruction error is reported separately.

`PreparedSlab.predict(alpha)` returns temperature arrays. `bottom_downward_gradient(alpha).gradient_K_m` returns signed q/k=(Ttop-Tbottom)/L-sum(v_n*n*pi*cos(n*pi)/L). It is a derivative of the truncated field; derivative tails converge more slowly than temperature. Do not infer derivative accuracy from temperature convergence alone.

For signed interval energy, `bottom_interval_gradient(alpha).integral_K_s_m` uses the independent moment M=integral(xi*T dz)=L[Ttop/6+Tbottom/3+sum(v_n*(-1)^(n+1)/(n*pi))]. The diffusion equation implies integral(q/k dt)=integral((Ttop-Tbottom)/L dt)-Delta M/alpha. This evaluates the exact modal moment difference and linear-boundary integral; finite-mode moment error remains. Under changing boundaries, the direct finite-N derivative and the moment operator differ by the omitted projection of boundary-rate forcing; they agree in the continuum limit. The net signed integral is not a positive-only heat sum. Conductivity is not identified from temperature diffusivity alone.

`with_assumed_ice_bottom(...)` is a separate conditional extension: a caller-supplied ice depth below all real probes is appended at assumed0 C. Its provenance explicitly records `assumed_ice_Tm0`; `measured_domain_depths_m` becomes null. It cannot establish that the deepest measured probe is the ice interface. Neither interface flux nor field melt is independently validated by this constructor.

Verification: 13 tests cover steady signed gradients, analytic homogeneous sine decay and its integrated bottom flux, a manufactured moving-boundary ramp, 32/64/128 derivative convergence, independent Crank-Nicolson FV temperatures, input/provenance guards, and initialization. The temperature-only v1 source and regression arrays are retained; the recurrence refactor preserves bitwise temperatures for three fixed synthetic diffusivities. All formulas are classical conduction identities, not claimed as new mathematics.
