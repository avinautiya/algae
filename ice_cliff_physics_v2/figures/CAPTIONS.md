# Reproducible figure captions

**1. Analytic cumulative ice-heat error.** Classical Robin slab, zero initial temperature and fixed contact coefficients. All12 frozen constitutive cases shown at0.1 and1 diffusion times. Independent axes prevent the large RC error from hiding the11.78–16.41% early FV error. At1 diffusion time FV error is below0.0541%. Fixed-boundary reference agreement does not certify changing-geometry or field accuracy. The separate post-study early-time audit is retained in `results/early_time_audit/`.

**2. Matched covered-footprint contrasts.** Fixed-z footprint; all four predeclared capacity/source combinations. Differences are relative to32-cell/60-s resolved model, not observed melt. The no-exchange curve is a controlled intervention, not a professional model. Equal wet/dry absorbed source is imposed, not derived from equal weather. Source and material-capacity units are explicit. See `results/COMPARISONS.csv` for small refinement differences hidden by the large intervention scale.

**3. Height-resolved toe shape.** Final profiles for the two specified absorbed-source boundaries. Horizontal coordinate is retreat, vertical is physical elevation. Lossless insertion of wet and cover boundaries avoids averaging narrow basal patches. The covered layer remains at fixed initial elevations. Horizontal ledges are adiabatic; the geometric undercut does not predict collapse, face stability or cliff lifetime. True profile ice volume and basal routing proxy remain separate.

The thermal module also retains analytic-reference and closure-error figures. Field-temperature and ablation figures reside with their own input, split and calibration records; they must not be presented as coupled cliff validation.
