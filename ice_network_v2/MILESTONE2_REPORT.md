## 1. Thermal-budget discrepancy (ramp–step residual growing with refinement): EXPLAINED
Equation actually solved (scalarTransportFoam, steady, `bounded Gauss linearUpwind`): `Σ_f φ_f T_f − T_P Σ_f φ_f − Σ_f DT (snGrad T)_f |S_f| = 0` per cell. Summing over cells the interior faces cancel, leaving the global identity of `cfd/budget_audit.py` over boundary faces: inlet advective + inlet **conductive** + outlet advective (T_b = T_P) + outlet conductive (0, zeroGradient) + wall conductive (T_b = 0, δ = 1/(n·d)) + lid (symmetry, 0) − Σ T_P div φ (bounded term, ~1e-11 of P_in). Same reporting surfaces, mesh-independent normalisation P_in = |φ_inlet|·T_in.
| mesh (cells/H) | inlet conduction ÷ P_in | old ledger residual (step) | full residual R ÷ P_in, step (orthogonal) | R ÷ P_in, ramp (non-orth ≤ 63°) | wall heat ÷ P_in step / ramp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | −1.52e-4 | −1.6e-4 | 1.9e-11 | 1.1e-4 | 0.19711 / 0.19666 |
| 16 | −3.04e-4 | −3.1e-4 | 3.0e-11 | 5.4e-5 | 0.20231 / 0.20175 |
| 32 | −5.86e-4 | −5.9e-4 | 7.2e-11 | 2.7e-5 | 0.20481 / 0.20419 |
**Cause: postprocessing omitted axial conduction through the inlet face.** The inlet T = 1 meets the wall T = 0 at a corner; the inlet-face gradient scales ~1/dx, so the omitted term (≈ 1/Pe_H = 0.3 % scale) doubles per refinement exactly as the old residual did. Including it, the orthogonal step mesh closes to 2e-11–7e-11 of P_in (rounding/solver tolerance). The non-orthogonal ramp retains 1.1e-4 → 5.4e-5 → 2.7e-5 (first-order decay) attributed to the non-orthogonal wall-flux reconstruction (OpenFOAM `nonOrthDeltaCoeffs`; not separately proven). Absolute: P_in = 7.8e-9·8..32 per face-set; relative values above are the quantities to read.
Consequence for the comparison: step − ramp wall heat 4.5e-4…6.2e-4 of P_in exceeds the ramp budget residual at n = 32 (2.7e-5) but is below the mesh change of the wall heat itself (5e-3, then 2.5e-3 for n = 8→16→32): **the downstream-enthalpy difference remains below numerical uncertainty — negative/unresolved result retained.** Design note: the inlet-wall corner singularity should be removed in any follow-up (adiabatic upstream wall segment).

## 4. Liquid heat transport in the free-surface implementation: PASS (diffusion), PASS-with-caveat (advection)
`interThermalFoam`: T solved in **both phases** with one equation `∂(C_v T)/∂t + ∇·(T[C_w α_φ + C_a (φ − α_φ)]) − ∇·(k_α ∇T) = 0`, `C_v = αC_w + (1−α)C_a` [J/m³/K] with C_w = 4.217e6, C_a = 1005, `k_α = αk_w + (1−α)k_a` (0.561, 0.0257; arithmetic mean, a closure choice), α_φ the MULES water-volume flux — the same flux that advances α, so enthalpy and phase fraction are consistent. T does not feed back on momentum (no buoyancy, no density change). **Free-surface thermal condition: none imposed;** T and conductive flux are continuous through the 1–2-cell interface; with effusivity ratio √(kC)_a/√(kC)_w = 3.3e-3 the surface is near-adiabatic for water; no evaporation, radiation or air-side heat transfer. Not transferable from the rigid-lid benchmark.
T1: cosine mode in 5 cm of water at rest (flow frozen with `frozenFlow`, because the parasitic currents of the free surface (3e-4 m/s) otherwise give Pe ≈ 100 and destroy the diffusion test — recorded), adiabatic walls, 300 s: amplitude error 1.4e-3, 3.9e-4, 1.5e-4 (dy = 5, 2.5, 1.25 mm; order 1.8, 1.4); profile RMS 8.3e-4 → 1.1e-4 K; total energy conserved to the printed digits; air temperature follows the interface value (9.144 K vs 9.146 K expected), water mean leaks 3e-5 K to air; Courant 0.1 vs 0.2: identical. T2: uniform-flow advection of a Gaussian pulse (cyclic, water/air layers, one transit 2.5 s): water L2 error 8.3e-2, 3.7e-2, 1.4e-2 K (dx 10, 5, 2.5 mm; order ≈ 1.2–1.4; peak preserved 0.68→0.94 of 1), energy drift ≤ 2e-8, and the Courant halving at dx = 5 mm changes the error by 10 % (first-order Euler in time) — spatial and temporal errors are comparable at the middle resolution. The air-phase error is not meaningful (air diffuses 190× faster than the reference Gaussian assumes). Local flux vs global ledger: global energy closed; local interfacial flux not independently measured.

## 6. Melting in the proposed implementation: DONE for the 1-D kernel; coupling NOT DONE

## 13. One experiment now enabled — and what would falsify the mechanism
**Matched-history thermal-memory test in the verified laminar rigid-lid solver** (fixed geometry, single step, Re_H = 50, Pr = 7): run the same step with two *physically attainable* upstream thermal developments (A: uniform inlet T with a long cold upstream wall, B: inlet T profile of a developed thermal layer scaled so that depth, flux and flow-weighted bulk T at the step section match A within a predeclared 1 %), with the corner-singularity fix of §1, three meshes (n = 8/16/32; est. 4 cases × ~15 min = ~1 h on 4 cores), measuring downstream wall heat flux q(x) and integrated heat to 10 residence lengths. Competing explanations it separates: (H1) local-equilibrium closure — wall flux depends only on current depth/flux/bulk T/geometry; (H2) thermal-state memory — the upstream profile moments matter; (H3) a purely geometric separation/reattachment effect (present in both A and B). **The proposed mechanism (state memory needed beyond the local closure) is falsified if the A–B difference in integrated wall heat over the 10 H after the step is below the mesh uncertainty at n = 32 (here ≈ 1 % of the integrated wall heat, set by the 5e-3-level mesh change observed) or decays to below it within ~2 residence times**; then the simpler local closure is kept. It does not test turbulence, free-surface effects or melting feedback.
