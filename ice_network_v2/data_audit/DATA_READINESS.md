## What supports a forward test and what does not
* **Forcing:** hourly channel discharge and lake discharge exist (derived, with std files) — usable as *prescribed forcing*, labelled as such. Inlet temperature is not separately defined: `temp*` are near-bed point sensors at P1/P2/P3/P5 — they must not be compared with a model bulk temperature or used as inlet bulk temperature without an observation operator (not built).
* **Geometry:** five stations of floor elevation with ±0.5 m at a handful of dates and a 50 cm DEM of the lake region; no cross-sections or longitudinal profile at forecast resolution. Observed daily incision is centimetres to decimetres, i.e. at or below the stated vertical uncertainty → **a forward test of bed lowering is not supported**. Later elevations cannot be inputs when they are the target.
* **Plausible, narrower test:** reach-integrated cooling (temperature drop between stations) and hourly Q → requires bulk-vs-near-bed operator, sensor timing/calibration checks (not done), and channel geometry assumptions.
* **Not confirmed:** time zone/clock conventions of each logger, calibration files, quality flags, completeness (notebooks not run; products contain NaN for early hours), whether P-stations match the CTD serials (Salt_injections hints 205309/207265 at P2/P1).
Verdict: **inventory complete; data not yet "ready" for a forward bed-lowering prediction.** Heat-export/cooling comparison is the only candidate and needs the operator work above.

## Supplement (milestone 3): what an independent temperature/drainage comparison would need
Counted from `products/*_hour.csv` (valid = non-NaN): hourly near-bed temperature `temp1/2/3/5` (stations P1, P2, P3, P5): 948 / 717 / 1216 / 989 valid hours, 2019-07-09…09-04 (P2 only to 08-08); hourly stage `stage1/2/3/5`: 671 / 382 / 970 / 813; channel discharge 970 valid hours (+ a std_dev file; its meaning is not defined in the README). **Metadata defect:** the `stageN_hour.csv` files carry the column header `Temperature_hourly` although they hold stage — column labels cannot be trusted without the notebooks.
Station chainage (m from P5): P4 151.3, P3 253.7, P2 312.6, P1 464.4 (`Stations_P5toP1.csv`; coordinates in CH1903 with decimal commas; P4 has no hourly temperature product).
Observation operator: **not constructible from the recovered metadata.** It would need the sensor's height above the bed and the local velocity/temperature profile (to relate a near-bed point reading to flux-weighted bulk temperature), the processing applied to `temp*_corr_raw`→`temp*_hour` (corrections, filtering, clock), and the inlet (lake-outlet) temperature definition. None of these are in the archive's README; the notebooks (not run) might contain some. Therefore **no model-versus-sensor temperature score was computed**, and the Nusselt products (`Nusselt_*`, built from the same stage/discharge/temperature series) are not used as independent evidence.
What is possible without an operator: (i) a sensor-free consistency check of discharge forcing vs lake volume loss (`lake_data/time_vol_2019.csv`, `discharge_2019.csv`) — a drainage-volume comparison, independent of temperature; (ii) a qualitative lag/attenuation comparison of near-bed temperature between P5→P3→P1, labelled as such. Neither tests bed lowering at centimetre scale (floor elevation ±0.5 m at ≤ 6 dates).
