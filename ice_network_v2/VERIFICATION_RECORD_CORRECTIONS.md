# Corrections and qualifications to the milestone-2 verification record (applies to MILESTONE2_REPORT.md at 2500517)

## C1. Stefan/ALE kernel: two different ledgers, reported separately
**Discrete energy closure** (sensible storage + conductive loss through the interface face = hot-boundary supply; every term taken from the numerical equation): residual ÷ supply = 1e-14 … 1e-12 for nx = 20–80 in all 24 runs; **finest-grid exception**: nx = 160, Ste 0.00631, residual = 1.4e-8 of supply (absolute 6.7e-3 J/m² against 4e-7 at nx = 80), whereas nx = 160, Ste 0.2 gives 1.6e-12. **Cause identified from the solver log:** at nx = 160 the linear solver (`smoothSolver`, symGaussSeidel, absolute tolerance 1e-16 which is unattainable) stops at the 1000-iteration cap in every one of the 4800 solves with final normalised residuals 5e-12 … 1e-15, whereas the nx = 80 solves converge; the energy ledger then accumulates the unconverged-solve imbalance. It is an iterative-solver artefact, not a discretisation error; a direct (tridiagonal) solver or a relative tolerance with a higher cap would remove it (a rerun with maxIter = 1e5 was started and not finished; the claim rests on the log evidence above).
**Physical sensible-plus-latent balance** (latent energy ρ_i L Δs against hot-boundary supply minus sensible storage): residual ÷ latent = 4.25e-4 at dt = 2.5 s, 8.5e-4 at 5 s, 1.70e-3 at 10 s (nx = 40; Ste 0.00631 and 0.2, equal and unequal density: identical to 3 digits). It is **proportional to dt (first order)** and **independent of nx and Ste** (4.253e-4 … 4.259e-4 for nx = 20 … 160). It is the difference between the interface speed integrated with the trapezoid rule (Heun) and the conductive loss integrated with the end-point (Euler) flux; it does not decrease under spatial refinement and decreases only with the time step. Both ledgers belong in the result: the discrete ledger shows the code is consistent with its own equations; the physical ledger shows an O(dt) bookkeeping inconsistency of 0.0425 % at dt = 2.5 s. The front-position error (first order in dx, 1.5e-5…1.1e-4) is insensitive to dt over 2.5–10 s.

## C2. Free-surface solver: what the benchmarks do and do not support
* Hydrostatic rest: passes on an **orthogonal stepped bed only**; the water-phase parasitic velocity **grows** with refinement (2.0e-5 → 4.2e-5 → 7.6e-5 m/s at dy = 12.5 → 6.25 → 3.1 mm). Not converged behaviour; unresolved.
* Curved (bump) beds **failed** (impulsive instability on non-orthogonal meshes).
* The T1 diffusion benchmark froze the flow (`frozenFlow`) because the free-surface parasitic currents (3e-4 m/s) would otherwise give Pe ≈ 100 and swamp heat diffusion. It is therefore an **isolated transport verification** of the temperature equation with alpha-weighted properties on a fixed phase field; it does **not** verify coupled free-surface flow + heat transfer. T2 (uniform-flow advection) shows time error ≈ space error at the middle resolution.
* Bore/jump: unresolved (depth error −33/−21/−12 %, time-step sensitivity 11 points); stationary-outlet jump failed.
* Coupled free-surface melting with moving ice was not built or verified. No statement about environmental melting follows from these tests.
