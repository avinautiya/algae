# FREEZE — matched-state upstream-thermal-history experiment (written BEFORE any step/straight heat-flux result was computed)

Question: does an unresolved difference in upstream thermal structure, at matched discharge, depth, velocity profile and flow-weighted bulk temperature, produce a downstream ice-directed heat-transfer difference that (a) exceeds the numerical error budget, (b) is missed by the local-equilibrium closure, and (c) is not already explained by ordinary developing-flow (Graetz/Lévêque) behaviour seen in a straight channel? Established background (not claimed as new): the downstream temperature of a Poiseuille flow depends on the upstream temperature profile through the Graetz eigenfunction coefficients; thermal-entrance behaviour after a sudden wall-temperature change (Lévêque, Prins, Mercer, Jones 1975 incl. axial conduction) and developing profiles are classical.

## Solver, geometry, flow (all frozen)
Stock OpenFOAM v1912: `simpleFoam` laminar (steady) → `scalarTransportFoam` (implicit Euler pseudo-time to steady state). 2-D, rigid free-slip adiabatic lid (declared proxy), Re_H = Ub H/ν = 50 (Ub = 5e-3 m/s, H = 0.01 m), Pr = 7, Pe_H = 350; uniform-velocity inlet at x = −16 H (flow develops before the thermal section; the same flow field is used for both thermal histories). Step geometry: upstream depth 1 H (x < 0), downstream depth 2 H, 40 H long. Straight control: depth 1 H throughout, 40 H long. Cold wall T_w = 0 (melting-point reference) everywhere it is "cold"; inlet T = 1 in units of the inlet excess; the problem is linear so histories are unit-inlet fields scaled afterwards.
Upstream bottom wall is split into five segments (x: −16…−13…−11…−9…−3…0). **Adiabatic upstream wall removes the inlet/wall corner**; the only temperature-discontinuity corner is at the start of the cold segment and lies ≥ 3 H upstream of the section in history B and ≥ −3 H (3 H upstream of the step) in history A, i.e. outside every measurement region (all observables are taken on x ≥ 0).
History A: cold wall from x = −3 H (short development). History B: cold wall from x = −11 H (long development). Both are physical (sudden-cooling at different distances). Sensitivity histories: B9 (from −9 H), B13 (from −13 H).

## Matching (exact by construction, then verified)
Same flow field ⇒ identical discharge, depth, velocity profile at the comparison section x_c = −1 H (identical to round-off; reported). B's inlet temperature is scaled so that the flow-weighted bulk temperature at x_c equals A's (T_b = Σ u T dy / Σ u dy over the section column of cells). Residual mismatches reported: profile-shape difference (the thing being tested), wall-gradient difference at x_c, bulk mismatch after scaling (round-off). Control perturbations bounding mismatch effects: (i) matching at x_c = −0.5 H and −2 H instead of −1 H; (ii) B scaled by (1 ± 10⁻³) (linear response); (iii) alternative histories B9/B13.

## Observables (definitions frozen)
Bed = all cold wall faces with face-centre x ≥ 0 (the step face at x = 0 belongs to the bed in the step geometry). Q_h(X) = Σ q·len over bed faces with x ≤ X, q = α T_P δ per face (T_w = 0, δ = OpenFOAM boundary delta coefficient; per unit depth), h ∈ {A, B*} (B* = B matched). Primary: δ_X = (Q_A(X) − Q_B*(X)) / ½(Q_A(X) + Q_B*(X)) for X = 10 H and 30 H. Secondary: spatial error e_q = ∫|q_A − q_B*| dx / ∫ q̄ dx over 0 ≤ x ≤ 30 H; downstream outlet flow-weighted T_b difference. Both for step and straight geometries (straight-channel Q over the same x range). Step-specific excess Δδ_X = δ_X(step) − δ_X(straight).
## Baselines (frozen)
L (local equilibrium, geometry-aware but history-blind): marching bulk-T with a fully developed Nusselt number computed numerically for the film profile (one wall isothermal, adiabatic free-slip lid); by construction it predicts δ_X = 0 for matched bulk T. D (classical developing-flow): (i) a Graetz-eigenseries solution for the straight upstream section checks the resolved solution's thermal development (history A and B at x_c); (ii) its prediction of the history effect downstream of the step is the straight-channel resolved δ_X (geometry-blind, history-aware). Cost: wall-clock of each (resolved solves vs closed-form marching).
## Error budget (per observable, computed, not assumed)
U² = u_mesh² + u_dt² + u_iter² + u_match² + u_loc², with u_mesh = |value(n=32) − value(n=16)| (and Richardson only if the three-mesh sequence is monotone), u_dt = spread over dt ∈ {5, 10, 20} s at n=16, u_iter = change over the final pseudo-time window, u_match = spread over matching planes and ±10⁻³ scaling, u_loc = spread over history B9/B13 vs B and over outlet length (28 H vs 40 H) and upstream length (inlet −16 H vs −24 H) at n=16.
## Decision rule (frozen)
(1) |δ_X| ≤ U(δ_X): history effect unresolved/negligible for that observable and horizon (stated as such; decay alone does not disprove memory). (2) |δ_X| > U but |Δδ_X| ≤ U(Δδ_X): ordinary developing-flow history effect, reproduced by the straight-channel baseline D; no step-specific claim. (3) |Δδ_X| > U(Δδ_X) and L and D both miss it: a step-specific history effect; only then develop the smallest additional thermal-state model and test it on withheld histories (withheld = B-start positions not used in fitting). If (3) is not met, no model is developed.
