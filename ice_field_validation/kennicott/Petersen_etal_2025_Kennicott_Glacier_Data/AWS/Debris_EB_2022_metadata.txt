Metadata for Meteorological Data Measured at Debris_EB_2022Data for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.AWS_Debris_2022Operated by UAF/Eric PetersenMay 29 - September 12, 2022Latitude: 61.4830 NLongitude: 142.9109 WElevation: 559 m a.s.l.All measurements were recorded to a Campbell Scientific CR1000 datalogger. Instrument readings were taken at 5 minute sampling intervals and averaged to 60 minute intervals for data recording.DATA FIELDS:Date_Time: UTC date and time presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format.T_2m: Air Temperature measured at 2 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured on a Vaisala HMP60 mounted in a Campbell Scientific radiation shield. Manufacturer stated accuracy
of ±0.6 degrees Celsius.RH_2m: Relative Humidity measured at 2 m height, reported as %. Measured on a Vaisala 
HMP60, with a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2% (<80% RH) to ±5% (>80% RH).T_3m: Air Temperature measured at 3 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured on a Vaisala HMP60 mounted in a Campbell Scientific radiation shield. Manufacturer stated accuracy
of ±0.6 degrees Celsius.RH_3m: Relative Humidity measured at 3 m height, reported as %. Measured on a Vaisala 
HMP60, with a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2% (<80% RH) to ±5% (>80% RH).Wspd: Wind speed measured at 2 m height, reported in m/s. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5% of total measured wind speed.Wdir: Wind direction measured at 2 m height, reported in compass degrees. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5 compass degrees.SWin: Shortwave incoming radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.LWin: Longwave incoming radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.LWout: Longwave outgoing radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.Tilt_West_deg: AWS mast tilt towards the west (negative values indicating an tilt to the east) measured by a Gefran GIT dual axis inclinometer mounted to the NR01 net radiometer. This 
value can be used to correct radiation measurements as the station tilts on the unstable 
debris-covered glacier surface. Stated manufacturer accuracy of ±0.15% full scale,
or 0.26 degrees.Tilt_South_deg: AWS mast tilt towards the south (negative values indicating an tilt to the north) measured by a Gefran GIT dual axis inclinometer mounted to the NR01 net radiometer. This 
value can be used to correct radiation measurements as the station tilts on the unstable 
debris-covered glacier surface. Stated manufacturer accuracy of ±0.15% full scale,
or 0.26 degrees.G1: Heat flux measured near the base of the debris layer (debris-ice interface), reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux HFP01, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of -15% to +5%.
18 cm debris thickness.G2: Heat flux measured near the base of the debris layer (debris-ice interface), reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux HFP01, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of -15% to +5%.
19 cm debris thickness. This heat flux plate was sandwiched between two thermistors, 
with temperature reported in the fields T_debris_G2_base and T_debris_G2_top. Effective 
thermistor spacing ~1.5-2 cm.G3: Heat flux measured near the base of the debris layer (debris-ice interface), reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux HFP01, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of -15% to +5%. 
17 cm debris thickness.T_debris_G2_base: Temperature measured at the base of the debris layer, underneath the G2 heat flux plate, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured using a BetaTherm 100K6A1IA Thermistor,
which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±0.2 degrees Celsius.T_debris_G2_top: Debris temperature measured overtop the G2 heat flux plate, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured using a BetaTherm 100K6A1IA Thermistor, which has a manufacturer 
stated accuracy of ±0.2 degrees Celsius. Estimated ~1.5-2 cm distance between T_debris_G2_top and T_debris_G2_base.VWC_debris: Volumetric water content reported as a decimal fraction. Measured on a Campbell Scientific CS655, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±3% volumetric water content.

Date_Time_ADT: presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format. The time zone is Alaska Daylight Time (ADT, -08:00).
