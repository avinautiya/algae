Metadata for Meteorological Data Measured at Debris_EB_2021Data for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.AWS_Debris_2021Operated by UAF/Eric PetersenJune 3 - September 11, 2021Latitude: 61.4803 NLongitude: 142.9057 WElevation: 546 m a.s.l.All measurements were recorded to a Campbell Scientific CR1000 datalogger, with the exception of measurements on 3 independent Onset Hobo U23 T/RH data loggers installed in the debris layer. Instrument readings were taken at 5 minute sampling intervals and averaged to 60 minute intervals for data recording. Note: Due to a wiring error on the Hukseflux NR01 instrument, measurements are not available for shortwave reflected radiation. DATA FIELDS:Date_Time: UTC date and time presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format.T_2m: Air Temperature measured at 2 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured on a Vaisala HMP60 mounted in a Campbell Scientific radiation shield. Manufacturer stated accuracy
of ±0.6 degrees Celsius.RH_2m: Relative Humidity measured at 2 m height, reported as %. Measured on a Vaisala 
HMP60, with a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2% (<80% RH) to ±5% (>80% RH).Wspd: Wind speed measured at 2 m height, reported in m/s. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5% of total measured wind speed.Wdir: Wind direction measured at 2 m height, reported in compass degrees. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5 compass degrees.SWin: Shortwave incoming radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.LWin: Longwave incoming radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.LWout: Longwave outgoing radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.Tilt_West_deg: AWS mast tilt towards the west (negative values indicating an tilt to the east) measured by a Gefran GIT dual axis inclinometer mounted to the NR01 net radiometer. This 
value can be used to correct radiation measurements as the station tilts on the unstable 
debris-covered glacier surface. Stated manufacturer accuracy of ±0.15% full scale,
or 0.26 degrees.Tilt_South_deg: AWS mast tilt towards the south (negative values indicating an tilt to the north) measured by a Gefran GIT dual axis inclinometer mounted to the NR01 net radiometer. This 
value can be used to correct radiation measurements as the station tilts on the unstable 
debris-covered glacier surface. Stated manufacturer accuracy of ±0.15% full scale,
or 0.26 degrees.G1: Heat flux measured near the base of the debris layer (debris-ice interface), reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux HFP01, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of -15% to +5%.G2: Heat flux measured near the base of the debris layer (debris-ice interface), reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux HFP01, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of -15% to +5%.G3: Heat flux measured near the base of the debris layer (debris-ice interface), reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux HFP01, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of -15% to +5%.T_debris_1: Temperature measured in the near-subsurface of the debris layer, recorded in degrees Celsius. Measured and recorded on an Onset Hobo U23 T/RH sensor/datalogger, which
has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±0.2 degrees Celsius.RH_debris_1: Relative humidity measured in the near-subsurface of the debris layer, recorded in %. Measured and recorded on an Onset Hobo U23 T/RH sensor/datalogger, which has a 
manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2.5% (10-90% RH) and ±5% (<10% and >90% RH).T_debris_2: Temperature measured in the near-subsurface of the debris layer, recorded in degrees Celsius. Measured and recorded on an Onset Hobo U23 T/RH sensor/datalogger, which
has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±0.2 degrees Celsius.RH_debris_2: Relative humidity measured in the near-subsurface of the debris layer, recorded in %. Measured and recorded on an Onset Hobo U23 T/RH sensor/datalogger, which has a 
manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2.5% (10-90% RH) and ±5% (<10% and >90% RH).T_debris_3: Temperature measured in the near-subsurface of the debris layer, recorded in degrees Celsius. Measured and recorded on an Onset Hobo U23 T/RH sensor/datalogger, which
has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±0.2 degrees Celsius.RH_debris_3: Relative humidity measured in the near-subsurface of the debris layer, recorded in %. Measured and recorded on an Onset Hobo U23 T/RH sensor/datalogger, which has a 
manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2.5% (10-90% RH) and ±5% (<10% and >90% RH).Date_Time_ADT: presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format. The time zone is Alaska Daylight Time (ADT, -08:00).
