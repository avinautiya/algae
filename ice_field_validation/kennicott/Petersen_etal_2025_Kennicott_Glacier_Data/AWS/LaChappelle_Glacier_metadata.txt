Metadata for Meteorological Data Measured at LaChappelle GlacierData for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.LaChappelle Glacier (Chinese Academy of Sciences AWS 2)Operated by CAS/UAF/Eric PetersenJune 12, 2019 - October 19, 2021Latitude: 61.6830 NLongitude: 143.0899 WElevation: 2200 m a.s.l.MEASUREMENT DESCRIPTIONThe station is located on a ridge above LaChapelle Glacier, a major tributary glacier to Kennicott Glacier. All measurements were recorded on a Campbell Scientific CR6 datalogger. Measurements were taken from the instruments at 10 second intervals and then averaged to 10 minute intervals for recording. We further downsampled the dataset to hourly intervals. Instrument heights for temperature/humidity and wind sensors refer to height above the snow-free ground.FIELD VISITSThe station was visited by helicopter 1-2 times per year (not possible in some years due to weather):4 June 2022 (visited by Eric Petersen, Philipp Arndt, and Joel Wilner)-	Station was leaning 17 degrees to the south due to a bent mast damaged by the wind.-	Battery was found to be dead; no replacement was on hand so plans were made to replace the battery and solar regulator on a subsequent visit. Station died on October 19, 2021 and this is the last data reported in this data paper.10 September 2021 (visited by Eric Petersen, Maria Zeitz, and Kitrea Takata-Glushkoff)-	Station was found in good health; data offloaded.  7 June 2021 (visited by Eric Petersen, Cameron Markovsky, and Julian Dann)-	Found that the solar power regulator had failed and was not providing power to the station. The battery and solar panels were in good condition, so a replacement solar regulator got the station up and running again.11 September 2020 (visited by Eric Petersen and Nicole Trenholm):-	Lower temperature sensor and wind speed monitor´s propeller were found barely out of the snow. Snow was removed to access the battery box.-	Station had no power; battery and top solar panel were replaced and power restored.29 June 2020 (photographed from helicopter by Mike Loso):-	Lower temperature sensor not visible (covered by snow) and wind speed meter partially covered in snow (propeller a few cm above the snow surface and back end of sensor just covered by snow).-	Top solar panel observed to have disappeared, likely through removal by force from the wind.   6 September 2019 (visited by Wanqin Guo, Haidong Han)-	Station was found to have fallen over, with the power system still working. Estimated time of mast failure from the instrument readings was 19 August 2019, 01:00.-	Radiometer was broken and therefore removed. Other sensors were in good condition. Re-installed the station.12 June 2019 (visited by Wanqin Guo, Haidong Han, and Donghui Shangguan)-	Station was installed. -	First data available at 18:20, 12 June 2019.DATA FIELDS:Date_Time: UTC date and time presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format.T_1_5m: Air Temperature measured at 1.5 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured by a Vaisala HMP60. Data removed from the record when the instrument was inferred to be buried by snow as indicated from unrealistic variability of the temperature measurements. Manufacturer 
stated accuracy of ±0.6 degrees Celsius.RH_1_5m: Relative Humidity measured at 1.5 m height, reported as %. Measured on an HMP60. Data removed from the record when the instrument was inferred to be buried by snow.
Manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2% (<80% RH) to ±5% (>80% RH).T_3m: Air Temperature measured at 3 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured on an HMP60. Manufacturer stated accuracy of ±0.6 degrees Celsius.RH_3m: Relative Humidity measured at 3 m height, reported as %. Measured on an HMP60.
Manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2% (<80% RH) to ±5% (>80% RH).Wspd_1_5m: Wind speed measured at 1.5 m height, reported in m/s. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5% of total measured 
wind speed. Data removed from the record when the instrument was inferred to be buried by snow.Wspd_3m: Wind speed measured at 3 m height, reported in m/s. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5% of total measured wind speed.Wdir_3m: Wind direction measured at 3 m height, reported in compass degrees. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5 compass degrees.Wdir_std_3m: standard deviation of wind direction of the 10 second measurement over the measurement period (1 hour) at 3 m height, reported in compass degrees. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5 compass degrees.Pressure: atmospheric pressure reported in hPa, measured by a Campbell Scientific CS106 Pressure Transducer located in the CR1000 datalogger box, with a manufacturer stated
accuracy of ±1.5 hPa.

Date_Time_ADT: presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format. The time zone is Alaska Daylight Time (ADT, -08:00).
