Metadata for Meteorological Data Measured at Debris Air Temp StationsData for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.Operated by UAF/PetersenAir temperature and relative humidity measured at 3 locations over debris-covered ice  The station was designed so that the sensor kept approximately the same sensor height relative to the debris surface. The locations are summarized below:Debris_T123 June 2020 to 8 September 2021Latitude: 61.4532 NLongitude: 142.9220 WElevation: 418 mDebris_T219 June 2020 to 2 September 2023Latitude: 61.4833 NLongitude: -142.9201 WElevation: 574 mDebris_T319 June 2020 to 2 September 2023Latitude: 61.4841 NLongitude: -142.9237 WElevation: 585 mThe rigging for Debris_T1 was damaged and bent over in Fall 2020, subsequently being buried in snow over the winter before being righted at its next visit on 9 June 2021. Thus data from September 2020 to June 2021 was removed for this sensor. Debris_T2 and Debris_T3 recorded data continuously through the study period.DATA FIELDS:Date_Time: UTC date and time presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format.T_1_75m: Air Temperature measured at 1.75 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured on an Onset Hobo U23 housed within an Onset Hobo RS1 radiation shield. The sensor has a
manufacturer stated accuracy of ±0.2 degrees Celsius.RH_1_75m: Relative Humidity measured at 1.75 m height, reported as %. Measured on an Onset Hobo U23 housed within an Onset Hobo RS1 radiation shield. The sensor has a 
manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2.5% (10-90% RH) and ±5% (<10% and >90% RH).

Date_Time_ADT: presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format. The time zone is Alaska Daylight Time (ADT, -08:00).
