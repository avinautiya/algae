Metadata for Meteorological Data Measured at "Gates 3600" SiteData for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.Operated by the National Park Service (NPS)/LosoAir temperature measured from June 11 to September 8, 2021.Latitude: 61.59907 NLongitude: 142.99937 WElevation: 1074 m a.s.l. Note that the instrument position is approximate because the glacier surface was flowing downstream and also lowering (due to ablation) during the melt season. Coordinates given were measured on June 11 and are projected to NAD 1983 UTM zone 7N with elevations in geoidal heights as meters above sea level. At all times the instrument was 2 meters above the glacier surface (whether snow or ice). This position was maintained by mounting the sensor and radiation shield onto a 2 meter PVC pipe that sat loosely over a metal stake drilled into the glacier. The PVC-mounted sensor was therefore able to drop down over time as the surface melted.DATA FIELDS:Date_Time: UTC date and time presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format.T_2_0m: Air Temperature measured at 2.00 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured on an Onset Hobo Tidbit temperature probe and data logger, contained within a non-aspirated Onset RS1 radiation shield. Manufacturer stated accuracy of ±0.2 degrees Celsius.Date_Time_ADT: presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format. The time zone is Alaska Daylight Time (ADT, -08:00).
