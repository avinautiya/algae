Metadata for Meteorological Data Measured at Debris_EB_2020Data for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.AWS_Debris_2020Operated by UAF/Eric PetersenJune 17 - August 21, 2020Latitude: 61.4841 NLongitude: 142.9237 W Elevation: 585 m a.s.l.All measurements were recorded to a Campbell Scientific CR1000 datalogger, with the exception of radiation components measured by a Hukseflux NR01 radiometer recorded to a CR5000 datalogger. Instrument readings were taken at 5 minute sampling intervals and averaged to 60 minute intervals for data recording. Periodic data outages occurred July 12-26 due to low battery voltage. A wind monitor was installed, however wind data are not reported here due to instrumentation issues.DATA FIELDS:Date_Time: UTC date and time presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format.T_2m: Air Temperature measured at 2 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured on a Young 43347-L RTD Temperature Probe mounted in a Campbell Scientific radiation shield.
The temperature sensor has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±0.3 degrees Celsius.T_3m: Air Temperature measured at 2 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured on a Vaisala HMP155 mounted in a Campbell Scientific radiation shield. The temperature sensor
has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±(0.226 - 0.0028 x temperature)°C.RH_3m: Relative Humidity measured at 2 m height, reported as %. Measured on a Vaisala HMP155 mounted in a Campbell Scientific radiation shield. The sensor has a manufacturer
stated accuracy of ±1%. SWin: Shortwave incoming radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.SWout: Shortwave reflected radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.LWin: Longwave incoming radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.LWout: Longwave outgoing radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.SRin: total solar incoming radiation (280 to 3000 nm) reported in W/m2, measured by 
Hukseflux LP02 pyranometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±10%.Pressure: Atmospheric pressure reported in hPa, measured by a Paroscientific Pressure Transducer located in the CR1000 datalogger box, which has a manufacturer stated 
accuracy of ±1 hPa.Date_Time_ADT: presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format. The time zone is Alaska Daylight Time (ADT, -08:00).
