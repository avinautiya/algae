Metadata for Meteorological Data Measured at Gates Glacier Automatic Weather StationData for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.Gates GlacierOperated by National Park Service, Central Alaska Network (CAKN)October 1, 2015 - October 17, 2023Latitude: 61.6029 NLongitude: 143.0132 WElevation: 1237 m a.s.l.MEASUREMENT DESCRIPTION:All measurements were recorded to a Campbell Scientific CR1000 datalogger. Measurements were taken from the instruments at 60 second intervals and then recorded (as average, maximum, etc) at 60 minute intervals for recording. Refer to Sousanes et al. (2019) for standard data processing and quality assurance applied to this dataset.Sousanes, P., K. Hill, M. Bower, W. Johnson, and S. Miller. 2019. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) 4: Climate Data Specifications, Sampling Frequency, and Measurement Intervals, Version 2.02. Weather and Climate Monitoring Protocol, Arctic (ARCN), Central Alaska (CAKN), and Southeast Alaska (SEAN) Inventory and Monitoring Networks. National Park Service, Fairbanks, Alaska. Available online at https://irma.nps.gov/DataStore/Reference/Profile/2259493.DATA FIELDS:Date_Time: UTC date and time presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format.T_2m: Air Temperature measured at 2 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured on a Vaisala HMP60 mounted in a Campbell Scientific radiation shield. Manufacturer stated 
accuracy of ±0.6 degrees Celsius.RH_2m: Relative Humidity measured at 2 m height, reported as %. Measured on a Vaisala HMP60, with a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2% (<80% RH) to ±5% (>80% RH).Wspd: Wind speed measured at ~2.5 m height, reported in m/s. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5% of total measured wind speed.Wdir: Wind direction measured at 2.5 m height, reported in compass degrees. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5 compass degrees.Wspd_peak: Peak wind speed measured over the hourly reporting window, reported in m/s. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5% 
of total measured wind speed.Wdir_peak: Wind direction during peak wind speed over the hourly reporting window, reported in compass degrees. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated 
accuracy of ±5 compass degrees.Solar_Rad: Incoming solar radiation, reported in W/m2. Measured using Li-Cor LI-200X Pyranometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5% of total radiation.Snow_depth: Snow depth reported in cm, measured using Campbell Scientific SR50A-L Ultrasonic Distance Sensor, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±2.5 cm.Precipitation: Liquid precipitation, reported in cm per hourly reporting window. Measured using Texas Electronics 525WS Tipping Bucket. Tipping bucket is not heated or shielded. Snow that accumulates in the bucket is recorded when it melts. Thus, data outside the summer seasons (June-August) is flagged as “suspect.” Manufacturer stated accuracy of ±3%.T_soil_10cm: Soil temperature measured at 10 cm depth, reported in degrees Celsius.
Measured using a Campbell Scientific 109 temperature probe, which has a manufacturer
stated accuracy of ±1 degree Celsius.T_soil_20cm: Soil temperature measured at 20 cm depth, reported in degrees Celsius.
Measured using a Campbell Scientific 109 temperature probe, which has a manufacturer
stated accuracy of ±1 degree Celsius.T_soil_50cm: Soil temperature measured at 50 cm depth, reported in degrees Celsius.
Measured using a Campbell Scientific 109 temperature probe, which has a manufacturer
stated accuracy of ±1 degree Celsius.Date_Time_ADT: presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format. The time zone is Alaska Daylight Time (ADT, -08:00).
