Metadata for Meteorological Data Measured at Amazon CreekData for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.Amazon Creek (Chinese Academy of Sciences AWS 1)Operated by CAS/UAF/Eric PetersenAugust 31, 2018 - September 5, 2023Latitude: 61.5110 NLongitude: 142.9025 WElevation: 682 m a.s.l.Data period: 31 August 2018, 14:00 - 5 September 2023, 11:00 (ADT, -08:00).MEASUREMENT DESCRIPTIONAll sensors were sampled at 10 second intervals. Then all the measurements except precipitation were averaged over 10 minutes intervals for storage on a Campbell Scientific CR6 datalogger. These data were converted to hourly and daily intervals by averaging. Precipitation was sampled and stored every 10 minutes, 1 hour and 1 day periods. Instrument heights for temperature/humidity and wind sensors refer to height above the snow-free ground. FIELD VISITSStation was visited approximately twice per year for data download and station maintenance which typically included emptying the rain gauge and adding antifreeze and oil.5 September 2023 (Visit by Eric Peterson, Regine Hock, Qiao Liu, Min Xu)-	Precipitation gauge was found not functioning and the sensor was removed.-	Radiometer arm was tilted downwards by 17 degrees, and the radiometer by 8 degrees; the arm was lifted so that the radiometer was leveled. Measured by a Rotronic HC2S3, with a manufacturer stated accuracy of ± 0.1 degrees Celsius. 
Temperature records were removed when the sensor was buried in snow.RH_1m: Relative Humidity measured at 1 m height, reported in %. Measured by a Rotronic HC2S3, with a manufacturer stated accuracy of ± 0.8% RH. RH records were removed when the sensor was buried in snow.T_3m: Air Temperature measured at 3 m height, reported in degrees Celsius. Measured by a Rotronic HC2S3, with a manufacturer stated accuracy of ± 0.1 degrees Celsius.RH_3m: Relative Humidity measured at 3 m height, reported in %. Measured by a Rotronic HC2S3, with a manufacturer stated accuracy of ± 0.8% RH.Wspd_3m: Wind speed measured at 3 m height, reported in m/s. Measured by RM Young Wind Monitor 05103, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of ±5% of total measured wind speed.SWin: Shortwave incoming radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer (www.hukseflux.com), which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of <5% of the total
measured shortwave radiation. The following correction was applied to SWin: whenever SWout was measured at a greater value than SWin, SWin was set to SWout divided by an albedo of 0.9.

SWout: Shortwave outgoing radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer (www.hukseflux.com), which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of <5% of the total
measured shortwave radiation.LWin: Longwave incoming radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of <10% of the total
measured longwave radiation. LWout: Longwave outgoing radiation reported in W/m2. Measured by Hukseflux NR01 net radiometer, which has a manufacturer stated accuracy of <10% of the total
measured longwave radiation. Pressure: atmospheric pressure reported in hPa, measured by a Campbell Scientific CS106 Pressure Transducer located in the CR1000 datalogger box, with a manufacturer stated
accuracy of ±1.5 hPa.RainSnow_Rate: precipitation rate reported in mm water equivalent per hour. Calculated from “RainSnow_Cum” measurements provided by Geonor T200B instrument (see below) as any positive difference in cumulative readings between hourly measurements. Negative differences 
were filtered from the record. Manufacturer stated accuracy of ±0.1% RainSnow_Cum: cumulative precipitation reported in mm water equivalent. Measured by a T200B (with accuracy of 0.1% full-scale, repeatability of 0.1 mm and sensitivity of 0.05 mm; www.geonor.com). The gauge has a protective housing with a WMO standard 200 cm2 inlet. The amount of precipitation is measured by weighing the mass in a bucket using vibrating wire load sensors. The instrument is mounted to a pedestal (inlet approx. 0.5 m above the snow-free ground). The gauge includes a standard 260-953 Alter-Type windscreen (122 cm in diameter, 41 cm leaf length, and 8 cm leaf width; www.campbellsci.com). The gauge is not electrically heated but antifreeze is added to melt any solid precipitation in the container. A thin layer of oil is added to impede any evaporation. The bucket was emptied and new antifreeze and oil added during field visits.

Date_Time_ADT: presented in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM format. The time zone is Alaska Daylight Time (ADT, -08:00).
