Metadata for Ice Cliff Backwasting DataData for Petersen, E., R. Hock, M. Loso, W. Guo, C. Markovsky, R. Yang, H. Han, D. Shangguan, and S. Kang, “Multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations on debris-covered Kennicott Glacier, Alaska, 2008-2023,” Geoscience Data Journal, Submitted January 2025.Ice cliff backwasting data is stored in .csv files organized into folders for the year of observation, and named for the ice cliff under investigation. The naming convention for the ice cliffs is P_IC_{cardinal direction ice cliff is facing}, for example 2022 data for the north facing ice cliff can be found in 2022/P_IC_N.csv. Data recorded in .csv files includes the date and time of observation and “D” denoting the length of ablation stake emerged horizontally from the ice cliff at that time, recorded in centimeters. Date/time field is in YYYY-mm-dd HH:MM:SS format, with 24-hour clock and time recorded in UTC (Datetime field) and Alaska Daylight Savings Time (Datetime_ADT field). 

Uncertainty on the length D of ablation stake emerged is estimated at plus or minus 2 cm and the 
uncertainty on timing at plus or minus 1 hour.Measurements of ice cliff slope and slope aspect are found in “ice_cliff_slope.csv” and “ice_cliff_aspect.csv” respectively, where each row represents an ice cliff observation site, each column represents the year of observation, and each value is the slope or slope aspect recorded in degrees.
