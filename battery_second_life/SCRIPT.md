## Facts to have ready if judges ask

- **Pegmatites** form from the last fluid-rich melt of cooling granite magma, which lets crystals such as spodumene grow large. The Greenbushes deposit in Western Australia is about 2.5 billion years old.
- **Brine:** in Chile's Salar de Atacama, in the driest non-polar desert on Earth, lithium-rich brine is pumped from under the salt flat and evaporated in ponds for many months.
- **Refining:** China handles roughly two thirds of the world's lithium chemical processing (IEA *Global Critical Minerals Outlook*).
- **Hydrometallurgical recycling** dissolves shredded battery material in acid solutions and then separates the metals. That's the same chemistry as natural weathering and leaching, only much faster.
- **Recovery rates and energy:** recovery of 90% or more of lithium, cobalt and nickel and up to about 80% less energy than primary supply are figures reported by recyclers. Present them as "up to" and "about".
- **Landfill:** lithium batteries in household waste are a known cause of garbage-truck and landfill fires.
