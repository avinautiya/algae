# Primary-source basis and reading scope

Reviewed 31 October 2026. These sources establish existing equations and prior art; they do not certify the proposed ice-channel contribution as new.

* [USACE HEC-RAS, Applications of the Momentum Equation](https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/confluence/rasdocs/ras1dtechref/7.0/theoretical-basis-for-one-dimensional-and-two-dimensional-hydrodynamic-calculations/1d-steady-flow-water-surface-profiles/applications-of-the-momentum-equation): official momentum-balance derivation and hydrostatic force assumptions inspected. Supports distinguishing momentum balance from head loss across a jump.
* [Clawpack Riemann book, Shallow water equations](https://www.clawpack.org/riemann_book/html/Shallow_water.html): governing equations, characteristic speeds, shock conditions and rarefaction structure inspected. The restricted initially stationary left-high/right-low problem is independently implemented here. No source code was copied. Its shallow-water assumptions must be respected when comparing to VOF.
* [AFiD-MuRPhFi, Stefan problems](https://chowland.github.io/AFiD-MuRPhFi/examples/stefan/): analytical similarity solution, inverse-Stefan convention and melting transformation inspected. Our convention is cp*DeltaT/L, the reciprocal of its stated latent/sensible parameter. Finite initial time avoids the singular startup flux.
* [Lethe, Stefan problem](https://chaos-polymtl.github.io/lethe/documentation/examples/multiphysics/stefan-problem/stefan-problem.html): official one-dimensional melting benchmark and model-refinement caveats inspected. This corroborates a benchmark target, not compatibility between Lethe and OpenFOAM modules.
* [Caruso et al. 2016, Physical Review E 93, 053110](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.93.053110), [author manuscript](https://iris.polito.it/bitstream/11583/2642897/1/PhysRevE.93.053110.pdf): primary introduction/method-summary passages inspected. Nonmodal amplification in unsteady sedimentary morphodynamics predates this project. Full equation-level comparison remains necessary before a related ice-specific claim.
* [Yokokawa et al. 2016, Cyclic steps on ice](https://eprints.lib.hokudai.ac.jp/repo/huscap/all/64599/Yokokawa_et_al-2016-Journal_of_Geophysical_Research__Earth_Surface.pdf): primary indexed analysis/limitation passages checked in this review, supplementing the earlier research packet. Ice-step instability and hydraulic jumps are established. A complete latest citation audit remains open.

The exact examples, numerical implementation and tests here are original project code implementing known mathematics. The scientific opportunity described in SCIENTIFIC_STUDY.md remains a hypothesis. A combination of familiar analysis tools is not automatically a new physical mechanism.
