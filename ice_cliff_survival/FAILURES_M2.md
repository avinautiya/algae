# Failures, parameter changes and dead ends recorded during M2 (nothing hidden)

1. **Ledger did not close with inert-wall junction faces (first coupled runs).** Floor nodes shared between ice and inert-wall faces move the junction faces; the default ALE flux then "created" liquid at the cell temperature there. Fix: treat all faces that can move (ice + junction faces of the inert walls) with the same local mass BC and zero enthalpy flux (liquid is created at T_m). Residual 0.85 J → 3e-7 J.
2. **Interface coupling did not converge (first step).** The limitedLinear convection limiter made the flux a non-smooth function of the geometry (bistable corner value, 2 %). Fixed by linearUpwind for T (linear in T given φ) and Aitken-relaxed fixed point. Coupling defect 4e-7 m → <1e-11 m.
3. **Ledger terms missing**: inlet/outlet diffusive heat (appears once the inert floors are isothermal at T_m), inert-floor conduction, and the advective heat through the lid in the lid-closure test. Added; discrete residual then ≈1e-13 of the terms.
4. **Exponential velocity instability at ρ_i ≠ ρ_w and later also with equal density once melt motion was present.** Initially attributed to the leading-edge scarp slope (hence a slope guard); bisected (ddtCorr off, upwind, smaller dt, 100× viscosity, no momentum convection: all unstable; momentum predictor off: stable; more PISO correctors delay it). Cause: PISO with the explicit-pressure momentum predictor at Δt ≫ viscous cell time amplifies a per-step mode (factor ≈ 6–20 per step, dt-independent). Resolution: **momentumPredictor off** (standard PISO option). The earlier "slope" explanation was wrong; the guard remains only as an admissibility check (default 1.0).
5. **PBiCGStab / PCG floating-point trap** on near-zero velocity fields (1-D unequal-density test) fixed with smoothSolver + relTol for U.
6. **Upstream wall**: with an adiabatic upstream wall the melt rate at the ice leading edge is singular (thermal entrance) and a steep scarp forms; the inert floors were changed to isothermal T_m (non-melting) so the thermal boundary layer is developed when it reaches the melting section. This is a case-design choice (it makes the result depend on the upstream length Lu; see r7), recorded here as a changed parameter, not tuned to produce an effect.
7. **Finest mesh aborted by the slope guard at 0.3** (r6, Δx=2 mm, t=1372 s): guard raised to 1.0 after the instability was traced to PISO (item 4), not to slope.
8. **Stefan-limit first attempts** failed with FPE in the U/p solvers (see 5) and by the same PISO instability.
