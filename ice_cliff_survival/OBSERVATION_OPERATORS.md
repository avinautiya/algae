# Observation operators: what each pinned product can be compared with

| product (record) | actual content (audited, AUDIT.json) | model output it can test | operator / required extra geometry | cannot be used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kennicott backwasting stakes (14625691) | 13 site-year CSVs, 6 named stakes (N, S, E, W, W2, SE), 2021–2023, 304 non-blank observations (309 rows including 5 blank rows), irregular intervals (median gap 21–44 h in the 10 densely sampled site-years; 3 site-years have only 2–3 observations spread over weeks, e.g. 2021 W, 2022 S; maximum gaps up to ~70 days), D = emerged horizontal stake length [cm] (±2 cm, ±1 h), one slope and aspect per site-year | horizontal recession rate of a cliff face at a stake; with the face slope, normal recession | normal rate = (dD/dt) sinθ (`observation.normal_recession_from_horizontal`, tested on a planar face); needs θ at the stake in time (archive: one value per site-year) | exposed-ice energy balance (it includes undercut, collapse, shade); stream power (no co-located discharge/temperature); independent samples: ≤ 6 cliffs, 13 time series, not 304 |
| Stream discharge gauges (10812353) | 9 experiments listed, 8 with data (gauge 4 experiment 2 folder missing), 5 gauges, 13–17 Jul 2021; transects with 3 profiles, floats; discharge from profile area × float speed reproduces 0.061–0.210 m³ s⁻¹ with surface-float speed (no depth-mean factor) | reach discharge forcing at five points and two days | `forcing.gauge_discharge`; surface-to-mean factor = parameter | multi-day or seasonal forcing; stream temperature (none); co-location with the stake cliffs (separate sites) |
| Ice-cliff inventory (10812353) | 10,306 cliffs, 2018 DEM-derived slope/aspect/area; **slope_min > slope_max in 100 % of rows** (labels swapped or mislabelled; unusable as written) | cross-sectional stream-adjacency contrast of cliff geometry | mapping error 4 % FP/FN; stream mapping 74 % true positive, 6–25 % over-estimated | survival (single epoch); time-resolved exposure |
| ArcticDEM cliff profile, 100 ramp profiles (10812353) | one cliff along 177 points in 2013/14/17/18; 100 NPS-DEM profiles (50 stream, 50 plain) | ψ/θ/φ contrast; one multi-epoch cliff | profile geometry; 2–3 m horizontal and DEM vertical co-registration uncertainty | exposure area through time for a population |
| Debris thickness, thermistor profiles, point mass balance, AWS, lake levels (14625691) | point debris thickness (cm), thermistor profiles, AWS (20 files) | upper-mantle thickness d_top, atmospheric forcing | point values only; no co-location guarantee | grain flux; burial |
Rules enforced in code: no interpolation of unavailable forcing; irregular intervals kept (`interval_rates`); horizontal vs normal retreat distinct; stream discharge, water temperature and sediment supply are never assumed co-located with a stake cliff.
