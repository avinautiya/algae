# Decisive comparison plan (specification only; nothing here has been run)

**Target observable (primary):** persistence of cliff-toe exposure over a declared horizon (binary: ice face still exposed and in stream contact at the second epoch) and exposed area through time; secondary: normal recession and integrated ice loss. **Data gap:** the pinned public archives contain one cliff-inventory epoch (2018) for 10,306 cliffs, a four-epoch profile for one cliff, and stakes at ≤ 6 faces without co-located stream forcing; a population survival test is **not possible with them**. A multi-epoch cliff inventory with stream positions (e.g. DEM epochs) or in situ stream monitoring at stake cliffs must be sourced first; until then only component verification and mechanism tests are possible.
**Baselines (strongest applicable):** (B1) Evatt-type no-stream critical-debris-thickness criterion; (B2) a *fairly trained* empirical classifier (logistic/GAM) on the same permitted inputs (stream distance/adjacency, slope, aspect, area, debris thickness), fitted on training glaciers/blocks only; (B3) a Buri-style parametrised reburial with a fitted rate; (B4) the structural stock model of this package with closures fitted on training data. **Information:** every model receives the same epoch-1 geometry, debris thickness and stream mask; the dynamic model additionally receives forcing it can use physically (discharge, temperature), the empirical models receive what is available for them; no epoch-2 information.
**Uncertainties:** stakes ±2 cm, ±1 h; DEM horizontal 2–3 m; cliff-mapping FP/FN 4 %; stream mapping 6–25 % over-estimate; single slope per site-year; cluster-level (glacier/block) confidence intervals, never per-pixel/per-observation.
**Exclusions/failure handling:** cliffs affected by crevasses, ponds, collapse or ice-flow acceleration flagged (Petersen §5.3) and reported with and without; failed or unconverged component runs kept and reported.
**Falsification of the proposed contribution:** (i) the dynamic model does not beat B2 on held-out blocks by a margin set before testing from the observational and numerical uncertainty; (ii) predicted survival is insensitive to stream heat D relative to the export capacity C (the data cannot separate the two), or the sensitivities (a)–(b) of §6 show saturation so that the model reduces to B1/B3; (iii) the budget/ledger or monotonicity tests fail in a coupled solver; (iv) an effect (warming reversal, hysteresis) appears only through an arbitrary closure.
**Stages (kept distinct):** component verification (done in part, `verify`); computational mechanism tests (next campaign); environmental observation comparisons (blocked by data); practical forecasting (not claimed).
**Figures planned (none rendered):** conservation/verification, exposure regimes (supply vs export), resolved flow–heat–grain behaviour, recession/area histories, held-out comparison.
