**Distinction claimed (methodological, not physical):** dynamically predicting cliff-toe exposure from conserved stream heat, an evolving interface and coarse-debris delivery/removal on a solid-volume ledger, with the monotonicity restrictions of §6 as falsifiers. Stream undercutting, debris export, cliff maintenance and abandonment, critical debris thickness and reburial by debris are **already** described qualitatively or parametrically in the works above; faster retreat supplying more debris is not independently new.
