Branch `claude/ice-cliff-survival-m0` (from a680fae). Environmental objective kept in view (stream-maintained cliff exposure, recession, melt); **this milestone does not establish cliff survival, warming reversal or forecasting superiority.** All case inputs below are ASSUMED test values, not measurements.

## 1. M1 defects fixed (details and old values: `CORRECTIONS.md`; tests `tests/test_m2_fixes.py`)
(1) debris normal thickness = (z_f−z) tan(θ−β)/sinθ (ray–line tested; M1 value kept as deprecated, wedge example rerun: 1 h mean advance 2.384 → 2.425 mm); (2) NaN/inf rejected in every record field, tolerances validated, diagnostics separated from the acceptance gate; (3) `neumann_fixture` equal-density only, hot-wall heat/liquid storage/front heat from independent analytic expressions (quadrature tests); `solve_lambda` checked and not defective; (4) `ToeRun` dt/horizon validation; (5) restart identity bound to registry closures + forcing contents, opaque callables refused or flagged unverified. 76 package tests pass.

## 2. Static shield (`SHIELD_DERIVATION.md`, `shield_planar.py`)
k/d eliminates the debris layer (massless, normal conduction); meltwater is created at T_m so its enthalpy flux relative to the T_m reference is zero by assumption, independent of any ledger closure (neglected pore advection: Péclet ≈ 2e-3). Resolved static two-material planar conduction (analytic series, validated against an independent Crank–Nicolson FV to ≤1e-5): the massless resistance over-delivers heat by ρc d T_w/6 (relative deficit → t_lag/t, t_lag = ρc d²/(6k)): d = 7 mm, k=0.5, ρc=2.5e6: t_lag 41 s (1.1 % after 1 h); d = 2 cm: 333 s (9 % after 1 h); d = 10 cm: the approximation fails (97 % deficit after 1 h). It does not validate the wedge-geometry (lateral conduction) assumption.

## 3. The coupled solver (`FLOW_STEFAN_DERIVATION.md`, `cfd/flowStefanFoam`, `cfd/flowstefan.py`, `python -m ice_cliff_survival flow-stefan`)
Laminar single-phase rigid-lid channel, one melting temperate-ice floor (graph, columns stretched between floor and lid), prescribed Poiseuille inlet (fixed Q) and temperature, outlet p=0 / zero-gradient T (documented), inert isothermal-T_m walls upstream/downstream. ALE PISO (momentum predictor off — see failures), conservative sensible-heat transport, backward-Euler Aitken-relaxed fixed-point interface coupling. Interface-normal liquid velocity: **local, per face**, u_w·S_f = (1−ρ_i/ρ_w) meshPhi_f (Stage A equal density, Stage B ρ_i=917; both completed). No global uniform-inflow closure at the interface (only the 1-D unequal-density Stefan TEST uses a global closure, at the *lid*, labelled test-only). Unsupported and explicit: shield + density jump (the drawn-out liquid's enthalpy at T_f ≠ T_m is not accounted; `flow-stefan --shield --rho-i 917` exits with UnsupportedRun).

## 5. Failures and changed parameters
See `FAILURES_M2.md` (PISO momentum-predictor instability — first mis-attributed to scarp slope —, ledger omissions, limiter-induced non-convergence, upstream-wall change, slope-guard abort, solver traps). The slope guard (default 1.0) remains as an admissibility check; a finite-height scarp forms where the melting floor meets the non-melting wall and steepens under refinement.

## 6. What works / what is unsupported
Works (laminar rigid lid, bare ice, throughflow, equal and unequal density, local mass condition, conservative energy, consistent time-integrated interface heat, verified against Stefan and Graetz references). Static shield only for equal density. **Unsupported:** mobile grains, free surface, turbulence, buoyancy, cold ice, atmosphere, fracture, glacier mechanics, wedge geometry beyond uniform-h patches, environmental forcing; the leading-edge/junction scarp beyond slopeMax. Wall-flux Graetz error floor ≈0.2 % is unexplained. These results do not support any claim about survival of ice cliffs.
