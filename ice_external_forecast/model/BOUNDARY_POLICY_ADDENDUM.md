# Pre-score boundary/control policy addendum

No Rochemelon bed targets are scored by this adapter. The parent match audit currently does not establish the inlet-to-target route or exact correspondence of the unlabeled Figure6 bed series to a surveyed cross-section. These remain refusal gates, not fitted choices.

Atmospheric heat input and frictional dissipation are **omitted component assumptions** for every family. If a source independently supplies a section's potential drop, report `rho_w*g*Q*drop` as a gravitational-power **scale**, and its ratio to inlet sensible-heat power where defined. It is not inserted into the heat budget or claimed to equal frictional dissipation. No observed future bed lowering is used to construct that diagnostic.

A rectangular critical-flow depth `d_c=(Q²/(g*w²))^(1/3)` may be used only at a verified critical breach control with independently sourced width, control location and inlet-to-control path. This is a source-backed model closure, not a measured depth. It cannot supply the unknown depth of an entire upstream channel.

The adapter distinguishes finite uniform reaches from zero-area **point controls**. A point-control target receives the temperature delivered through all specified upstream reaches, has no finite added area/heat removal, and reports local bed heat flux and vertical thermal recession per unit area. This is the finite-patch-to-zero-area observation limit, not total melt volume. It may match a fixed surveyed bed point only after the parent records the point/path alignment. An unobserved upstream prefix remains a refusal: setting its length to zero is permitted only when the source audit verifies that the water-temperature boundary and control are coincident.

Input metadata is a declared evidence contract, not software proof that a survey exists. The executable verifies required metadata and numbers; the parent matching audit remains responsible for source truth. Synthetic verification packets are explicitly stamped synthetic. There is no built-in Rochemelon geometry or external result.
