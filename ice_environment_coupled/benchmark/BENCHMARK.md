# Glacier cliff environmental-coupling benchmark

Status: **development-suite protocol, 1 November 2026**. No professional model was run, no archive downloaded, and no independent predictive skill is demonstrated here. Cases and thresholds below are proposed engineering choices, not measured environmental ranges or a preregistered unseen test.

## Comparison to implement

Question: does prognostic hydraulic contact improve cliff evolution relative to a prescribed-contact closure when geometry, atmospheric forcing, water heat supply, numerics, and calibration budget are matched?

Use one shared energy/geometry driver and interchangeable contact modules. Let `C(x,t)` be wetted/contact fraction; distinguish it from heat-transfer coefficient and water temperature. Dynamic contact derives C from storage/head, spill/drainage and evolving geometry. Prescribed contact uses an initial fixed mask or an independently specified time series. A prescribed replay of dynamic C is an implementation control, not a competitor with independent information.

Use two experiments. (A) Freeze geometry and replay identical water and atmospheric input energies: isolate contact allocation/closure. (B) Evolve identical initial geometry with the same update law, debris lowering, reburial rule, and timestep: measure coupled feedback. Identical geometry is required initially and for experiment A; forcing evolved geometries to remain equal in B would remove the effect being studied. Report both absolute ice loss and cliff retreat relative to the lowering debris surface.

Water energy must be a finite shared reservoir or a shared prescribed heat-flux budget. Compute water-to-ice energy once; debit water enthalpy by exactly that transfer. Do not add `rho*c*Q*DeltaT` as melt while also applying an independent contact melt flux funded by the same water. Log advected enthalpy, atmospheric exchange, storage change, outflow and latent heat. Dry cells cannot receive contact heat; zero water superheat cannot yield positive water-driven melt. Separate mechanical loss from thermal melt.

## Professional references and access evidence

- [Buri et al. 2016, JGR, DOI 10.1002/2016JF004039](https://doi.org/10.1002/2016JF004039): evolving 3-D cliff geometry, debris reburial and parameterized pond influence provide the appropriate architectural comparison. Pond influence used prescribed subaqueous melt and affected area; unmodeled water-level variability was a stated limitation. Thus extending hydraulic contact is a testable closure change, not an established correction to all cliff physics.
- [Buri and Pellicciotti 2018, PNAS](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1713892115): aspect-dependent survival motivates paired aspect experiments. Their simulated equator-facing decay is a conditional process result, not a universal ranking for every season/site.
- [Evatt et al. 2024, Annals](https://doi.org/10.1017/aog.2024.34): a canonical energy-balance model uses a frame moving with surrounding debris-covered ice and Baltoro parameterization. Its illuminated-aspect result should be compared only after matching illumination, debris melt and retreat definition. It does not supply an independent hydraulic-contact truth model. An open paper is verified; executable code/data access was not established here.
- [Sato et al. 2025, Journal of Glaciology](https://doi.org/10.1017/jog.2025.17): Trakarding application resolves seasonal forcing and geometry; aspect dependence weakens toward monsoon. Its AWS/debris-temperature processing motivates seasonally coherent radiation and temperature inputs. The paper alone is not evidence that full raw observations or its complete model are openly downloadable.
- [CliffEBM public repository](https://github.com/pburi/CliffEBM) and [official 2023 addendum](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/annals-of-glaciology/article/gridbased-model-of-backwasting-of-supraglacial-ice-cliffs-on-debriscovered-glaciers-addendum/2195E24B7DC78A964EEC8E7D5F1F57E1): verified public R energy-balance code, GPL-3.0, example Lirung DEM/shapefile/meteorological input and archived DOI [10.5281/zenodo.7970338](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7970338). This is a bounded candidate for atmospheric-energy comparison, **not verified availability of the complete evolving 3-D JGR model**. Pin a commit, audit dependencies and units, then reproduce its supplied example before adapting it.
- [Petersen et al. 2024 stream-hydrology study](https://esurf.copernicus.org/articles/12/727/2024/) explicitly links products at [10.5281/zenodo.8373145](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8373145). This is evidence of an open product deposit, not an assertion that it contains colocated discharge, temperature and contact time series sufficient for the proposed skill test. Inspect metadata before acquisition.

## Inputs and bounded suite

Acquire at most one example cliff and one short, contiguous forcing segment after metadata inspection. Retain timestamps, units, missingness and sensor provenance. Use source DEM/topography for slope, aspect, shading and view factors; measured/source meteorology for air temperature, humidity, wind and incoming radiation; documented debris properties and surface temperature for longwave exchange. Water supply, head, temperature, drainage and contact histories must have their own provenance. Meteorology cannot establish these hydraulic quantities. Do not infer water temperature from air temperature without a labeled, sensitivity-tested closure.

Until these observations are available, use the **dimensionless synthetic** suite in the JSON. It tests bookkeeping and mechanism only: zero water heat, constant contact, pulsed supply, drainage disconnection, and geometry feedback. Aspect/season experiments require actual source forcing or a clearly labeled idealized solar construction; do not manufacture independent measured ranges for radiation, discharge, pond temperature or debris thickness. Scale dimensional inputs only after provenance is recorded.

For each contact pair hold fixed: mesh, initial geometry, timestep, meteorology, upstream supply and enthalpy, outlet law except the specified contact intervention, radiative/turbulent closures, density/latent heat, debris properties, reburial, calibration data and objective. The initial-mask baseline receives only initial contact; a measured-history baseline receives measured contact and is a distinct, more informed competitor. Do not fit either to evaluation loss. Include no-water ablation and dynamic-contact replay controls. Count each paired scenario once, not each mesh cell as independent evidence.

## Metrics, acceptance and interpretation

Store per-step mass/energy balances, wetted area, water storage, outflow, contact heat, melt volume, normal retreat, slope/aspect distribution and cliff footprint. Report signed bias and MAE for measured quantities only; specify ice volume versus water equivalent. For observed geometry report registration/measurement uncertainty and footprint precision/recall/F1. Benchmark-to-benchmark discrepancy has no observational error score. Report wall time and parameters requiring calibration.

Numerical gate: nonnegative storage; contact in [0,1]; no transfer beyond available reservoir energy; cumulative energy residual <= 1e-6 of total absolute energy throughput (synthetic engineering target); refine timestep by two and require integrated melt change <=1% for smooth cases. Discontinuous events require event-time convergence reporting, rather than forcing that criterion. Failing a gate blocks scientific interpretation.

A surrogate driven by the same prescribed contact/energy law can demonstrate **closure consistency** only. Agreement with CliffEBM demonstrates atmospheric-energy implementation agreement within its scope. A dynamic-versus-prescribed difference demonstrates sensitivity, not improvement. Independent skill requires separately observed time-resolved contact/head, water supply/temperature and registered geometry/melt, uncertainty propagation, and evaluation cases excluded from tuning. Current development cases remain disclosed and reusable. Freeze a later protocol and acquire genuinely withheld cases before any confirmatory claim; do not rename this suite an unseen test after tuning. Report failures and parameter sensitivity with successes. No inference of general superiority, universal aspect behavior or field validation is licensed by this protocol alone.
