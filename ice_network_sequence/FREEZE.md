# Freeze of the sequence-effect test (written and committed before any test case is run)

**Development (S01-S10, 90 network runs, 10 RTM_seq predictions) is complete and informed the criterion.** Findings used: (i) RTM_seq reproduces the network's AB-BA effects (end share within 0.003 in 9 of 10 cases); (ii) the closed-form heat-difference eta (`theory_eta.py`) reproduces the network's heat-to-ice sequence difference within 0.01 in 8 of 10 cases; (iii) the product rule delta_share ~ eta x dphi_inc captures the thermal-storage cases but not the geometry-driven ones (S04, S08).
**Frozen predictors:** RTM_seq (17-station tabulation, `rtm_seq.py`), eta (`theory_eta.py`), the product rule, and the classification below; all equations, constants and tabulations are those of the commit that contains this file.
**Test configurations:** X01-X10 in `sdesign.TEST` (chosen by predicted-effect stratum from `scan_rtm.json`, 120 RTM-only draws; two low-effect cases, three medium, two near-tie, two high; one low case is a degenerate-symmetric duplicate removal: scan entry 117 was excluded as an artefact of exact symmetry). Predictions: `TEST_PREDICTIONS.json/.md` (sha256 in `TEST_PREDICTIONS.sha256`).
**Experiment definition (all cases):** same initial state (lake stage 0.10 m above sill 1; lake temperature = discharge-weighted mean temperature of the two episodes; undisturbed channels); episodes A and B of equal duration tau; common continuation of 6 h at the discharge-weighted mean forcing; raised-cosine ramps of 900 s applied to Q and to the heat flux Q T so that water (integral Q dt) and sensible heat (integral Q T dt) are identical for AB, BA and the averaged control to 1e-13 (verified in `forcing.py`); averaged control: constant Q_bar and discharge-weighted T_bar over the whole duration. Surface heat exchange is excluded (the model has none); lake heat exchange with the atmosphere is excluded; meltwater is not re-added to the lake in the reduced reduction.
**Runtime estimate before launch (112 cases):** network r17 (60 runs, 12-30 h simulated) about 45 min serial; r33 (6) about 15 min; resolved reference 200 cells (18 runs) about 50 min; 400 cells (6 runs) about 80 min; closure (18) about 9 min; tolerance check (4) about 2 min: about 200 min serial, about 100 min on two workers. No resolution is reduced; reference 200/400 and network r17/r33 are the convergence evidence.
**Metrics:** per outlet cumulative delivered water; instantaneous partition share_end = Q2/(Q1+Q2) at the end of the continuation (and at the start of the continuation, for persistence); dominant destination (instantaneous and cumulative) with tie = |share - 0.5| < 0.03; switching occurrence/time (first Q2 > Q1); sill lowering of each route; heat delivered to the ice; final lake stage; conservation residuals; effect = AB - BA; averaging error = AVG - AB, AVG - BA.
**Practical effect thresholds (routing):** a sequence effect on routing is *meaningful* if any of: destination flip (dominant outlet differs between AB and BA, both non-tie); |delta share_end| >= 0.05; |delta cumulative delivered share| >= 0.03; switching occurrence differs. An *incision-only* effect: sill lowering differs by >= 15 % on either route or heat to the ice by >= 10 %. **Numerical resolution rule:** an effect is *resolved* only if it exceeds three times the numerical uncertainty u of the same quantity, u = |effect(r17) - effect(r33)| (network) or |effect(ref200) - effect(ref400)| (reference); otherwise it is reported as unresolved. For the closure and tolerance tests the same effect definition is used.
**Ablations:** fixed geometry (evolving storage only: lake level and lake temperature, no incision), dissipation-only incision (development only), thermal network; closure multipliers nu_factor 0.5 and 2 on three configurations; corrected energy tolerance (atol_energy 1e-3 J) against the legacy setting on two configurations. Equal incoming budgets do not imply equal heat delivered to the ice: the final lake temperature and the heat delivered to the ice are reported.
**Comparators:** averaged-forcing control, fixed geometry, resolved reference (200 and 400 cells) on X02, X04, X07, X08, X09, X10 (X04, X07, X10 at 400 cells).
**Scope:** RTM_seq is a reduction of the production kernels, not independent physics; the near-boundary wrong-sign case of the previous milestone (T02 with 33 stations) remains unresolved; closures are uncertain; nothing here establishes real-glacier accuracy. If a defect is found the test version stays as published and a new independent assessment is required.
