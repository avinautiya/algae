## What is therefore established about novelty
Nothing is established. Candidate novelty is limited to a specific, bounded combination: matched water and sensible-heat budgets (discharge-weighted mean temperature), reversed episode order with a common continuation, a decomposition into reservoir-timing, thermal-storage and geometry terms, and the finding of whether such order effects reach outlet selection. Each ingredient, taken separately, is either standard (Lie-bracket expansion, averaging error of nonlinear laws) or partly present (time-varying flux in a single channel: Jarosch & Gudmundsson 2012; history memory of a channel: Schoof et al. 2023).
