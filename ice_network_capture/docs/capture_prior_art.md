## What the existing literature already supplies
* melt-driven incision with positive flow-melt feedback (all dissipation-based; Roethlisberger/SaDS/Schoof/SHED);
* the single-channel runaway criterion (incision outpaces lake lowering);
* the qualitative expectation that of two outlets the larger persists (Schoof et al. 2023, stated as a presumption).

## What this work could add (and the uncertainty)
1. A **kinematic reduction**: for two outlets the sill gap obeys d(Delta)/dt = E1 - E2 and the share obeys a replicator-type equation with fitness r_i = d ln Q_i/dt|_H. Elementary; it is a re-expression of the single-channel criterion for two outlets and may well appear elsewhere (not found, not searched exhaustively).
2. A **capture threshold** Delta_c where incision rates are equal although flows are not, and its independence from inflow temperature and lake size under advected-heat incision (first order). Whether this has been stated is unknown.
3. A **closure-dependent stability contrast**: with the lake held fixed, advected-heat incision stabilises a symmetric pair (index < 0) while dissipation-driven incision destabilises it (index > 0). This is a numerical result on one rating family, not a theorem.
No priority claim is made: absence of an exact phrase in the sources read is not evidence of novelty.
