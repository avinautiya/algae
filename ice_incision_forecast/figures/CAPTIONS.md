# Reproducible method figures

All panels are synthetic conditional-model demonstrations.

**fig01_equal_outlet_different_incision.** Two admissible profiles attain opposite regional extrema while sharing outlet states. Shading marks the predeclared region. Power is for the first interval; lowering integrates the full horizon.

**fig02_sharp_spatial_envelope.** Pointwise bounds are marginal and cannot all be attained together. Sampled feasible profiles illustrate the class without defining a probability distribution.

**fig03_regional_forecast.** A single fixed witness attains each time-integrated extreme despite changing inlet scenarios and the common conductance multiplier. The midpoint minimizes scalar worst-case absolute error in this class. The declared incision threshold is not a flood threshold; no future outlet measurement is an input.

**fig04_thermal_information_limit.** Idealized exact prefix information partitions the uncertainty class. Real point sensors and noisy measurements require observation operators and uncertainty treatment; this is not a field sensor-performance claim.

**fig05_independent_checks.** Independent RK4 integration, exhaustive small-case extrema and the physical effect of meltwater addition check different parts of the implementation. Numerical agreement supports the implementation; the proof establishes the conditional theorem.

**fig06_local_vs_total_information.** Information sensitivity for the declared control and a secondary broader-reach observable. Bulk information can constrain a reach average while leaving local allocation uncertain. The second region is an explanatory scale comparison, not a replacement primary endpoint.
