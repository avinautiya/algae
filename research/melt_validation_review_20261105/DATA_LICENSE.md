# Third-party data attribution

Derived Pirámide2022–2023observations and their metadata are based on the official CC BY4.0dataset at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11581073 , "Calculating Supraglacial Debris Properties at Pirámide Glacier, Chile (Data Sets and Codes)". The saved11581073_metadata.json identifies the creators and published version. Cite the dataset and associated Melo-Velasco et al.2025paper https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JF008360 . License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

Changes made here: decode native timetables; select complete camera-endpoint days and meteorological windows under the documented criteria; preserve signed changes; compare frozen predictions; derive score tables and plots. The data creators do not endorse these analyses. Raw source files are downloaded separately from their official record and are not part of the release ZIP.

Older donor datasets are cited in OBSERVATIONS_AND_UNCERTAINTY.md. Their raw files and derived training feature table are not redistributed in this archive because explicit redistribution terms were absent from the metadata checked in this pass. Model parameters and aggregate comparison statements are supplied, with that reproducibility limitation stated. Do not infer a blanket license for every historical source from this package's MIT code license.
