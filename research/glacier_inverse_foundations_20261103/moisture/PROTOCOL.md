# Pre-outcome Tower2 estimator transfer test

All four previously inspected Tower1 periods are development data for this new estimator. Their failed earlier evaluations remain in the record. The global parameters are fitted to those four development targets only. The still-unopened Tower2 evaluation targets are a new stake test inside the same weather campaign, not a new glacier or prospective field trial.

Primary index: nonnegative air-temperature exposure times exp[beta*(lowest-probe moisture minus own-TRAIN reference)]. Local gain is fitted only to Tower2 December2–16 camera ablation, after global parameters/code and normalized exposures are published. Hyperparameter beta5.4 per unit volumetric fraction comes from Tower1 development. A signed-grid sensitivity check gives the same optimum; beta's sign is not a universal thermodynamic law.

Eight baselines remain: ordinary air index, buried-temperature index, positive near-basal-gradient index, constant rate, shifted-ratio moisture/air index, centered-softplus moisture/air index, exponential calendar trend/air index, and moisture-only exponential index. Learned baseline parameters use the same Tower1 development targets and one scalar global hyperparameter each. The shifted-ratio +0.031 is a fixed feature regularizer, not a repaired moisture observation. Centered softplus also cancels constant sensor offsets, so exponential offset immunity is not advertised as unique. Exponential calibrated ratios additionally do not depend on the chosen centering constant.

Four Tower2 evaluation periods are December16–30, December30–January13, January13–27, January27–February10, all exact03:00 source-local labels. Training is December2–16 at03:00. No camera interpolation or target deletion is permitted. Primary metric is mean absolute period error; all nine methods, period errors, signed biases and ties are retained. No confidence interval or independent-period claim is inferred.

Air exposure is the exact positive-part integral of piecewise-linear native TA3 segments, with finite brackets at most18h and no extrapolation. Moisture is held left over each native half-hour. All source rows are retained; operating-range and sensor flags are reported instead of selecting favorable periods. Geometry, probe depth and unknown source clock interpretation remain limitations.

Release sequence: publish initial source/specification freeze; calculate and publish normalized exposures; release only TRAIN camera endpoints; calculate and publish all calibrated predictions plus seal; release only EVALUATION endpoints; score once. A valid source reader must verify local artifact hashes and preserve the original raw-distance times917kg/m³ operator. No method/parameter/primary may change after the evaluation release.

This is an empirical positive melt index with exact additive-offset symmetry, not a conserved heat-flux solver or causal moisture-effect proof. Moisture may respond to melting, different sensors sample different depths, and independent drift or multiplicative calibration error remains. A conditional drift factor exp(±2*abs(beta)*delta) is a sensitivity statement, not an independently measured field confidence interval. Passing this test would support only the declared stake transfer against the declared baselines; it would not guarantee Nature publication or competition placement.
