## Query families used

* Ice cyclic steps, nonlinear stability, spectral stability, wavelength selection, 2024–2026 follow-ups.
* Cyclic steps on erodible beds, secondary instabilities, coarsening and roll waves.
* Hydraulic jumps, periodic Evans–Lopatinski analysis and moving discontinuities.
* Ice ripples/scallops, thermal diffusion, oscillatory melting and turbulent melt morphodynamics.
* Glacial conduit heat-transfer sources, transient closure validity and roughness.
* Himalayan supraglacial channels, Lirung/Khumbu drainage, field knickpoint evolution.

Representative exact searches included `"cyclic steps" "ice" "stability"`, `"cyclic steps" "stability" "eigenvalues"`, `"ice" "cyclic steps" "memory"`, and `"Cyclic Steps Created by Flowing Water on Ice Surface" stability`. An empty or irrelevant result is not proof that a topic is unstudied.

## Priority claims that are specifically prohibited at present

“First ice-step model”; “first coupled hydrodynamics and melting”; “first nonlinear cyclic-step stability theory”; “first model with thermal memory”; “new Evans/Floquet mathematics”; “first proof that discharge variability affects ice erosion.”

Permissible present wording: **a proposed stability-based test of thermal ice-step spacing and melt localization, with cold-ice response treated explicitly where its timescale warrants it**.

## Remaining literature work that could overturn the recommendation

Read the original Camporeale/Ridolfi and Parker/Izumi papers in full, inspect forward citations to Wu/Izumi and Yokokawa through the submission date, and check thermal/erosional pattern-selection work outside glaciology. Inspect the complete Johnson et al. spectral construction before implementing a determinant. This session establishes a concrete candidate gap; it does not certify an exhaustive literature review.
