Data corrections
The following steps were taken to quality control, correct or filter data.
[1] Shortwave Radiation
Incoming shortwave radiation (SWIN) was compared to the theoretical potential shortwave after transmission through the atmosphere under clear sky conditions, calculated following 
vectoral
 algebraic functions of 
Corripio
 (2003).   Any hourly SWIN value exceeding the theoretical value is corrected to that theoretical value.  < 2% of the total SWIN values were corrected in this way (Figure 1).
Figure 
1
: Comparison of theoretical (red) and measured (blue) SWIN for the 2014-2015 period
[2] Precipitation
Because no local data exist for precipitation within the basin and for the selected dates, ERA5 total precipitation values were utilised for the relevant grid cell corresponding to 
Pirámide
 Glacier. Values of ERA5 already exist in hourly format but are corrected using differences in daily totals when comparing to daily data at 
Embalse
 
Yeso
 AWS (~10 km horizontal distance) for the same time period.  Differences are averaged across the hourly data of ERA5 to reduce precipitation on average by 0.05 mm. Values corrected to

[3] Snow Height
Sonic depth measurements of snow height are filtered and corrected (Figure 2 top) using the following steps:
erroneous
 values of > 1 m increase in a two hour period are removed based upon inconsistencies with the amount of precipitation typical of the region.
Erroneous spikes of data > 0.25 m are removed if are present in periods <= two hours.
Small spikes (<0.25 m) are removed if they do not correspond to 
i
) cloudiness/shortwave radiation spikes ii) precipitation or iii) clear albedo rises associated with likelihood of precipitation falling.
Small noise is filtered with a low pass filter.
Values and noise <0.02 m are set to zero.
NOTE: Further filtering of erroneous data might be necessary to ensure consistency with albedo/presence of snow in satellite information.
