# Challenge set freeze
`challenge.json` (sha256 in `challenge.json.sha256`) was generated by `python campaign.py cases challenge` (Sobol, scrambled, seed 33, 32 points; each point run with reduced r9/dt900, r17/dt450 and reference 200 cells, every 4th point also reference 100 and 400) and committed BEFORE any challenge case was executed. Ranges (`CHALLENGE_RANGES` in `campaign.py`) lie outside the development envelope (`PARAM_RANGES`) on every axis and include: initially overflowing route 1 (negative deficit), route 2 as the lower sill (negative offset), pulsed inflow up to 90 % amplitude with periods from 15 min to 8 h, and slopes/discharges that push the reduced model outside its quasi-steady, subcritical regime. No case will be removed after the fact; failures are classified numerical / outside-regime / unexplained from the per-run applicability diagnostics.
Not covered (stated limitation): networks other than a lake with two parallel routes (three routes, cascades, junction merging): the evaluation harness supports exactly two routes.
